The Structure

Binary Network —
Your Two Streams of Income

Atomy uses a binary structure: two lines (A and B) grow beneath you. PV from every person in your entire network — with no depth limit — flows up to you.

YOU
Your account — center of both lines

A Line (Left)

Your first referral stream. All their purchases and their downline's purchases accumulate here.

B Line (Right)

Your second referral stream. Grows in parallel — both lines must grow for maximum income.
